Welcome

I'm Dr. Glenn Thompson, a volcano seismologist specializing in real-time monitoring systems, legacy data recovery, and machine learning for volcanic hazard assessment. My work integrates operational monitoring, software development, and international collaboration, particularly with observatories in the Caribbean, Latin America, and the US.

This site highlights my research, open-source tools, collaborations, and efforts to train the next generation of geophysical monitoring professionals through initiatives like FLO-VO and environmental seismology at USF.
